A patch for 0.1.0. rehalo no longer asks to re-pair a Halo that doesn't need it.

## Fixed

- **No more false re-pair prompts.** After closing and reopening the app, rehalo could ask to re-pair
  the Halo although its password was fine, and following the recovery gave the ring a new password.
  It now asks only when the ring refuses every password it knows, and it picks up the connection the
  previous launch left open.
- **The password stays the same.** A ring that lost its password, after an interrupted recovery for
  example, gets the saved one back without the recovery wizard. When a recovery is really needed, the
  ring gets the password already in your Keychain, so other iPhones and password managers that know it
  keep working.
- A rain or call alert that arrives while the app connects no longer breaks the connection.
- "Enter a saved password" keeps the current password until the ring accepts the new one.

## Install

Download `rehalo-unsigned.ipa` below and sideload it with your own Apple ID, using
[iLoader](https://github.com/nab138/iloader) over USB or [SideStore](https://sidestore.io). Needs
iOS 17 or later and a real iPhone. The [README](https://github.com/cyberlynx33/open-rehalo#install-the-ios-app)
has the details.

## Known limits

- If the app is closed in the second it takes to connect, the next launch can take up to about 20
  seconds to connect.
- Another app connected to the ring, such as the official SmartHalo app, can hold the connection for
  up to a minute. Uninstall it if you don't use it.
- The recovery wizard doesn't notice a reboot over USB: tap "I've rebooted it".
- Everything listed for [0.1.0](https://github.com/cyberlynx33/open-rehalo/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
