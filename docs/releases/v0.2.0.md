The second release of rehalo. It brings a new navigation engine with a wrong-way warning, stops the
false re-pair prompts, and fixes many smaller issues found by checking the app against the ring's
firmware.

## New

- **Navigation engine.** rehalo now places you on the whole route, not just the next stretch, so loops,
  out-and-backs, shortcuts and going back to a missed turn are followed properly, with far fewer false
  "off route" alerts. It's on by default. The previous engine is under Settings › Advanced › Classic
  navigation.
- **Wrong-way warning.** Riding the route the wrong way puts a U-turn on the ring, plays a sound and
  shows a banner to invert the route or ignore it. Inverting starts the guidance where you are.
- **Install and update from SideStore or AltStore.** Add the source
  `https://rehalo.app/altstore/apps.json` and new releases show up as updates.

## Fixed

- **No more false re-pair prompts.** After closing and reopening the app, rehalo could ask to re-pair
  the Halo although its password was fine, and following the recovery gave the ring a new password.
  It now asks only when the ring refuses every password it knows, and it picks up the connection the
  previous launch left open.
- **The password stays the same.** A ring that lost its password, after an interrupted recovery for
  example, gets the saved one back without the recovery wizard. When a recovery is really needed, the
  ring gets the password already in your Keychain, so other iPhones and password managers that know it
  keep working.
- **A steadier connection.** Commands wait their turn instead of failing when another one is in flight,
  so a rain or call alert no longer breaks the connection, and a dropped link no longer leaves the app
  waiting.
- **Front light:** brightness goes from 10 to 100 %, as the ring does, blinking works, and both survive
  a relaunch.
- **Options the ring never supported are gone:** compass calibration, Heartbeat, the Compass section
  and the Fitness intro. "Demo" is now "Alarm demo".
- **Navigation:** the ring shows the road's name instead of where the road leads. A loop you join part
  way through ends where it should, and stopping at the destination no longer holds the arrival. The
  compass pointer no longer goes wrong when you face just west of north. NAVIGATE and Compass need
  Location, and Navigating says so if it's turned off during a ride.
- **Routes:** importing a large GPX no longer freezes the app, and GPX files with prefixed elements
  import, named after their track.
- The Bluetooth permission prompt appears when you pair a Halo, not over the welcome screen.
- The Display, Fitness and server rides screens follow your language and units, with more of the app
  in Spanish.
- A server address without `http://` means `https://` unless it's on your home network, and the login
  screen warns when a server uses plain HTTP.
- Smaller fixes: the Map tab keeps its view when you come back to it, a rename the ring refuses keeps
  the old name, the Halo's name is limited to the 19 bytes the ring keeps, the alarm recap only shows
  when the alarm was on, and "Enter a saved password" keeps the current password until the ring accepts
  the new one.

## Install

**From SideStore or AltStore:** add the source `https://rehalo.app/altstore/apps.json` under Sources.

**Or** download `rehalo-unsigned.ipa` below and sideload it with your own Apple ID, using
[iLoader](https://github.com/nab138/iloader) over USB or [SideStore](https://sidestore.io). Needs
iOS 17 or later and a real iPhone. The [README](https://github.com/cyberlynx33/open-rehalo#install-the-ios-app)
has the details.

## Known limits

- The new navigation engine has been checked in simulation and on few rides. If it misbehaves, switch
  to the classic one, and turn on Settings › Advanced › Record rides to help tune it.
- If the app is closed in the second it takes to connect, the next launch can take up to about 20
  seconds to connect.
- Another app connected to the ring, such as the official SmartHalo app, can hold the connection for
  up to a minute. Uninstall it if you don't use it.
- The recovery wizard doesn't notice a reboot over USB: tap "I've rebooted it".
- Everything listed for [0.1.0](https://github.com/cyberlynx33/open-rehalo/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
